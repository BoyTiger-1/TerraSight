Scenario Simulator

What happens if the weather changes?

Pick a location and TerraSight loads its real current conditions as a baseline. Then drag the sliders, temperature, rainfall, wind, humidity, soil moisture, instability, snowpack, ocean heat, and every model instantly re-scores. Beyond the straight before-and-after, you can rank which variable this place is most exposed to, plot the full response curve of any model, and solve for exactly how far a variable has to move before a hazard crosses into High or Extreme.

Tip: the first load builds the baseline (about 20 to 40 seconds). After that, every slider change updates instantly.

Environmental knobs

Adjustments follow simple physics: evaporative demand rises about 5% per degree, storm fuel (CAPE) about 7% per degree, drying the rain out lengthens the days-since-rain clock, and warming a scenario past freezing removes its ice hours. Climatological baselines such as the local 90th-percentile heat threshold stay fixed, which is exactly what makes the comparison a what-if.
